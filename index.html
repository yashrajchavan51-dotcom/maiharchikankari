Our Story

Rooted in Lucknow,
at home in Thane.

Chikankari began centuries ago as delicate white-on-white needlework, worn by Mughal royalty on the finest muslin. That same patience and precision — every booti and jaali stitched by hand — is what we bring to every piece at Maihar Chikankari.

We work directly with artisans to keep the craft authentic, offering pieces that feel as considered for a wedding function as they do for a Sunday brunch.

The Craft

What makes it chikankari

A tradition of needlework passed down through generations of artisans, still practised almost exactly as it was centuries ago.

Hand-Traced Motifs

Every design is block-printed and then embroidered entirely by hand, booti by booti.

Jaali & Shadow Work

Fine net-like jaali stitches and shadow work give each piece its signature delicacy.

Artisan-Led

Sourced from artisan clusters who have practised this embroidery for generations.
